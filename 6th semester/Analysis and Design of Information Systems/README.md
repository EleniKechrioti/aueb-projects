# Information Systems Analysis and Design

This repository includes a series of six comprehensive exercise sets developed for the **Information Systems Analysis and Design** course (Academic Year 2023-2024).

## Coursework Structure
The assignments are structured as small-scale case studies and problem-solving exercises, focusing on the fundamental principles of system analysis. Key areas covered include:

* **System Identification:** Defining system boundaries, interfaces, and environmental influences for various organizational entities.
* **Requirements Engineering:** Distinguishing between descriptive and normative propositions, and classifying functional vs. non-functional requirements.
* **Problem Analysis:** Breaking down complex scenarios into manageable functional components and logical data flows.
* **Collaborative Design:** All exercises were executed in pairs, simulating real-world professional collaboration in system modeling.

## Methodology
Each set of exercises requires the application of theoretical frameworks to practical examples (e.g., self-service stations, auction systems, or parking assistance software), ensuring a deep understanding of how information systems are conceptualized and designed.
