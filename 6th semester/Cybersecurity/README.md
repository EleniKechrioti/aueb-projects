# The Contribution of Artificial Intelligence to Cybersecurity

This repository contains a research paper titled **"The Contribution of Artificial Intelligence to Cybersecurity"**, written for the **Information Systems Security** course at the Athens University of Economics and Business (April 2024).

## Abstract
This paper explores the multifaceted role of Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) in the cybersecurity landscape. It delves into the benefits of AI for threat detection, the potential risks posed by AI-driven attacks, and the critical ethical implications of its use.

## Key Topics Covered

### 1. AI as a Defensive Tool
* **Threat Detection & Analysis:** How AI workbenches and tools like VirusTotal Code Insight empower analysts to identify threats faster.
* **SOC Operations:** The role of AI in Security Operations Centers for managing complex environments.
* **Phishing & Malware Detection:** Using machine learning to identify malicious patterns and Zero-Day exploits.

### 2. AI as a Threat Vector
* **Automated Attacks:** The use of Generative AI by malicious actors to create sophisticated phishing content.
* **Polymorphic Attacks:** Discussion on advanced techniques such as Polymorphic Adversarial DDoS attacks using GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks).
* **Ransomware Detection:** Techniques like Honey Pots used to detect modern ransomware variants.

### 3. Ethics and Regulation
* **Global Landscape:** A look at the global landscape of AI ethics guidelines and the legal risks associated with algorithmic auditing.
* **Compliance:** The impact of regulations like the GDPR on AI implementation in security.
* **Detectors:** The emergence of tools like GPTZero and ZeroGPT to identify AI-generated content.
