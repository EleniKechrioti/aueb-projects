# Information Systems Analysis and Design - Coursework (2023-2024)

This repository contains a collection of homework assignments and major projects for the **Information Systems Analysis and Design** course at the Athens University of Economics and Business (AUEB). The work focuses on identifying system requirements, modeling business processes, and designing structural solutions for complex organizational problems.

## Coursework Overview

### Homework Assignments (HW1 & HW2)
The homework series focuses on the fundamental principles of systems thinking and requirements engineering.
* **System Identification:** Analyzing the boundaries, interfaces, and environmental interactions of systems such as self-service kiosks and automated parking assistants.
* **Requirements Classification:** Distinguishing between descriptive and normative propositions and identifying Functional vs. Non-Functional requirements.
* **Decision Analysis:** Evaluating system behavior through decision tables and logical flow for scenarios like electronic auction systems.
* **Process Modeling:** Breaking down organizational activities into structured sub-functions and identifying logical data stores.

### Major Projects (Project 1 & Project 2)
The projects involve the end-to-end analysis of a real-world business case: **"The Reading Nook"**, a specialized bookstore expanding into a hybrid physical and digital model.

#### Project 1: Analysis & Requirements
* **Goal:** Detailed recording of the current state and identification of new system needs.
* **Business Processes:** Analysis of procurement, inventory management, and customer sales (physical and online).
* **Deliverables:** High-level system requirements and organizational structure mapping.

#### Project 2: Modeling & Design
* **Goal:** Create formal models for the proposed Information System.
* **Context Diagrams:** Defining the system's interaction with external entities (Suppliers, Customers, Bank).

* **Data Flow Diagrams (DFDs):** Detailed hierarchical decomposition of processes (Level 0, Level 1) to show how information moves through the business.

* **Data Dictionary:** Formal definition of all data elements and structures used within the system.

## Methodology & Tools
* **Modeling Techniques:** Functional Decomposition, Decision Tables, and Structured Analysis (DFD).
* **Requirements Engineering:** Elicitation and categorization based on business goals.
* **Collaboration:** Assignments were performed in pairs to simulate professional systems analyst team environments.
