# ACCESS: Accessible City & Community Engagement Support System

## Project Overview
**ACCESS** is an innovative accessibility mapping platform aimed at improving the mobility of individuals with disabilities, the elderly, and parents with strollers in urban environments. The project combines crowdsourced data, real-time updates, and dedicated tools for municipalities to create a more inclusive city.

---

## Project Deliverables & Phases

### Phase 1: Problem Identification & Solution Concept
* **Problem Definition:** Focuses on the lack of reliable, real-time data regarding the condition of sidewalks and public spaces.
* **Core Idea:** Development of an application that provides digital guidance and allows users to report obstacles in real time.
* **Impact:** Aims to eliminate social and economic exclusion for individuals with mobility challenges.

### Phase 2: Market Research & Competition Analysis
* **Market Segmentation:** Identification of key users (People with disabilities, elderly, tourists) and primary customers (municipalities, local businesses).
* **Competitor Analysis:** Comparison with existing solutions (e.g., WheelMap, WheelMate). ACCESS differentiates itself through voice commands, dynamic route mapping, and a dedicated dashboard for municipal authorities.
* **Risk Assessment:** Analysis of risks such as data accuracy and competition from large-scale mapping companies.

### Phase 3: Business Model & Strategy
* **Business Model Canvas:** Analysis of key partners (municipalities, NGOs), revenue streams (municipal subscriptions, premium advertising), and distribution channels.
* **Key Activities:** Platform development, data analytics, and partnerships with "Smart City" initiatives.
* **Value Proposition:** Enhancing user independence and providing statistical data to municipalities for urban infrastructure improvements.

### Phase 4: Product Design & Prototyping
* **User Experience (UX):** Design of a customizable User Interface (UI) with an emphasis on ease of use.
* **Features:**
    * Secure login and incident reporting with photo uploads.
    * "Start" function for tracking and mapping accessible routes.
    * Municipality Dashboard for visualizing accessibility statistics.
* **Prototyping:** Development of interactive mockups using Figma.

### Phase 5: Final Pitch & Roadmap
* **Milestones:** 
    * May 2025: Completion of Proof of Concept and start of implementation.
    * August 2025: Completion of the Minimum Viable Product (MVP).
    * November 2025: Launch of the promotion and advertising phase.
* **Vision:** Transforming cities into environments designed for everyone through technological innovation.
