---

## Abstract

**ACCESS** is an innovative information system designed to transform urban mobility for people with disabilities and citizens with mobility challenges.  
The platform provides **real-time, reliable, and interactive accessibility data** for streets, public spaces, and businesses — helping users plan their daily routes more efficiently.

Users can:
- Access an **interactive accessibility map**.  
- **Report** and **view** accessibility issues in real-time.  
- Upload **photos and comments** to support reports.  
- Discover **accessible businesses** and **public facilities**.  

Municipal authorities will have access to a **web platform** that allows them to update the system with verified data and generate **statistical reports** on accessibility problems within their jurisdiction.

Ultimately, ACCESS is not just an app — it’s a **social inclusion tool**, supporting **voice commands**, **adaptive UI settings**, and **community collaboration** between citizens and local authorities.

---

## Objectives

The project aims to:
- Provide an **interactive and dynamic mapping system** for accessible routes.  
- Allow **citizens** and **municipalities** to contribute real-time data on accessibility.  
- Foster **inclusion** and **equal access** to urban life for people with disabilities.  
- Leverage **crowdsourcing** and **AI insights** for continuous data improvement.  
- Offer municipalities analytical tools to enhance urban planning and accessibility infrastructure.

---

## Project Scope and Expected Outcomes

### Functionality
- **Mobile App:** Personalized accounts, interactive map, route discovery, voice commands, dynamic UI adjustments, photo-based reporting.  
- **Web Platform:** Centralized data management for municipalities and public institutions.  
- **Real-Time Updates:** Accessibility changes (construction, maintenance, new ramps, etc.) shown instantly on the map.

### Benefits
- Reliable data for individuals with disabilities.  
- Decision-support insights for local authorities.  
- Increased visibility for accessibility-friendly businesses.  
- Empowerment of citizens through participatory data sharing.

---

## Market Need & Timing

The ACCESS solution responds to a **critical unmet need** in accessibility data aggregation and reliability.  
Existing platforms such as *Wheelmap* and *WheelMate* are limited — focusing only on static locations or offering incomplete coverage.

Given:
- 27% of EU citizens (aged 16+) and 23.1% of Greeks face some form of disability.  
- Accessibility laws such as the **European Accessibility Act (EAA)** and **EU Disability Strategy 2021–2030** are pushing cities to modernize.

There’s a strong opportunity for a **smart-city-level accessibility system**.  
Mobile adoption and geolocation APIs make this technically and socially *the perfect moment* to launch ACCESS.

## Risk Management

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|------|--------------|-------------|
| **Data Privacy** | Sensitive location data from users | Anonymization & full GDPR compliance |
| **False Accessibility Reports** | Inaccurate or outdated user data | Crowdsourced validation + municipal verification |
| **Low Adoption** | Few users submitting data | Partnerships with municipalities & disability orgs |
| **Technical Failures** | API downtime, scaling issues | Cloud redundancy, monitoring & recovery mechanisms |
