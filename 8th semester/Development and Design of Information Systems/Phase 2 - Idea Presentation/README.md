## Phase 2 – Presentation & Concept Development

### Objective
The goal of this phase was to **present the ACCESS concept** — a platform that enhances **urban accessibility** for people with disabilities and citizens with mobility challenges.  
The presentation focused on the **problem**, the **user needs**, and how **ACCESS** provides a **technological and social solution**.

---

### The Problem
- **1.3 billion people** worldwide face mobility issues.  
- Cities are **not designed for inclusivity**, especially for people with disabilities, elderly citizens, or parents with strollers.  
- There is **no reliable, real-time data** on the condition of sidewalks and public infrastructures.  
- Existing tools (e.g., Google Maps) do **not reflect accessibility barriers** or construction works in real time.

---

### Real User Scenario
**Anna**, a mother pushing a stroller, tries to reach a nearby park:  
- Encounters **broken sidewalks** and must walk on the road, risking safety.  
- **Google Maps** doesn’t reflect accessibility obstacles.  
- Her travel time increases and the experience becomes stressful.  

This case represents thousands of similar experiences in every city — proving the need for **live accessibility mapping**.

---

### Key Statistics
- **27%** of EU citizens with disabilities limit daily activities.  
- **23.1%** of people in Greece face similar mobility constraints.  
- **45%** of the population has reduced mobility (including elderly, parents with strollers, injured, or pregnant individuals).

Surveys also showed:
- 84% of respondents have **no personal disability**, but 16% have **a close relative** who does — demonstrating empathy and social awareness.  
- **70%+** of respondents use navigation apps frequently.  
- **98%** stated they would use or contribute to an app improving accessibility data.

---

### Proposed Solution

**ACCESS** aims to:
- Provide a **real-time interactive accessibility map**.  
- Collect **crowdsourced reports** (photos, comments, accessibility ratings).  
- Offer **voice control** and **adaptive UI** for users with disabilities.  
- Send **live notifications** when accessibility conditions change (e.g., construction works, temporary barriers).  
- Enable **municipal dashboards** with statistics and reports to improve infrastructure planning.

---

### Core Features

| Feature | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Interactive Map** | Real-time monitoring of sidewalks and routes. |
| **Crowdsourced Data** | Users add and verify accessible/inaccessible points. |
| **Photo & Comment Upload** | Contextualized reports for obstacles or facilities. |
| **Filters** | Search by type of accessibility, route difficulty, etc. |
| **Voice Commands** | Navigate and report hands-free. |
| **Municipal Dashboard** | Public authorities access aggregated data & analytics. |
